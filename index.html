🐸 Frog Pond Defense

Round0 / 50
Lives20
Flies (money)120
Shop
How to play
Pick from the shop (or press 1 / 2), then click the map to place it.
Click a frog or tadpole to open its upgrade menu.
3 paths: ⚡ Speed, 💥 Damage, 🎯 Range. Only one path can go past tier 2. Tier 4 unlocks a super attack – fire it from the Abilities bar on the left, or press Q to fire them all.
Survive all 50 rounds!
Stop the bugs reaching the pond!

Bugs
Tier 1 – Gnat (weak)
Tier 2 – Beetle (tanky)
Tier 3 – Hornet (fast + tough)
BOSS – Stag Beetle (super tanky, slow) – round 5, 8, then every 5 rounds
