How to play
Pick from the shop (number keys 1 – 9, 0 and -), then click the map to place it.
Click any of your towers to open its upgrade menu.
3 paths: ⚡ Speed, 💥 Damage, 🎯 Range. Only one path can go past tier 2. Tier 4 unlocks a super attack – fire it from the Abilities bar on the left, or press Q to fire them all.
Survive every round: Easy = 25, Medium = 40, Hard = 50. Freeplay never ends – see how far you can get! (You can also keep going in Freeplay after you win.)
Stop the bugs reaching the pond (or the frogs' home)!

💀 ULTRA BOSS – Giant Mole Cricket (round 50, Hard only)
Stage 1: a huge, slow, tanky bug. Stage 2 (at half health): it digs a tunnel. Every bug comes out of the tunnel, easy bugs keep pouring out, and it takes 25% less damage. Too big to stun!

Flying bosses cut corners, and fly straight across the pond on Frog Home!
