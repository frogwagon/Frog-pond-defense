🐸 Frog Pond Defense

1. Choose a difficulty

The host picks the map. Each player has their own flies and frogs; lives are shared.

2. Weather (optional: click to turn on, hover for details)

3. Choose a map to start!

How to play
Pick from the shop (number keys 1 – 9, 0 and -), then click the map to place it.
Click any of your towers to open its upgrade menu.
3 paths: ⚡ Speed, 💥 Damage, 🎯 Range. Only one path can go past tier 2. Tier 4 unlocks a super attack – fire it from the Abilities bar on the left, or press Q to fire them all.
Survive every round: Easy = 25, Medium = 40, Hard = 50. Boss Rush is only bosses, forever! Freeplay never ends – see how far you can get! (You can also keep going in Freeplay after you win.)
Stop the bugs reaching the pond, the frogs' home, or the exits!

Bugs (weakest to strongest)
1 – Cricket (hops, weakest)
2 – Gnat
3 – Mosquito (fast)
4 – Beetle (slow, tanky)
5 – Ladybug
6 – Fire Ant
7 – Hornet (fast)
8 – Praying Mantis
9 – Wasp (fast + tough, rare, from round 9)
10 – Rhino Beetle (strongest, from round 16)
Stink Bug (from round 12): when squashed, its stink stops nearby frogs attacking for 1.5 seconds
Pill Bug (from round 20): armored, blocks 25% of all damage

Bosses (weakest to strongest)
11 – Stag Beetle (slow) – new at round 5
12 – Giant Centipede – new at round 10
13 – Atlas Moth (flies) – new at round 15
🕷️ Wolf Spider – new at round 20: splits into 6 fast Spiderlings when squashed
14 – Yellow Jacket (fast, flies) – new at round 25
15 – Asian Giant Hornet (flies) – new at round 35
16 – Hercules Beetle (armored: blocks 30% of all damage, strongest) – new at round 45
Each boss shows up once at the end of its first round. 5 rounds later it starts coming in the middle of rounds, every few rounds, more often and in bigger groups later on.

💀 ULTRA BOSS – Giant Mole Cricket (only once: round 50, Hard)
Stage 1: a huge, slow, tanky bug. Stage 2 (at half health): it digs a tunnel. Every bug comes out of the tunnel, Beetles and Ladybugs pour out every half second plus a Rhino Beetle every 20 seconds, and it takes 25% less damage. Too big to stun!

Flying bosses cut corners, and fly straight across the pond on Frog Home!

👻 Invisible bugs (you can only see and hit them inside a Glass Frog's glow)
Glasswing Butterfly (flies) – first one at the end of round 30
Ghost Mantis (tough) – first one at the end of round 40

Saving: the game saves itself after every round. Use Continue in the menu to pick up where you left off.
Challenges: beat them for 🐸 Frog Tokens, then spend tokens on perks in the menu.
🏅 Challenge
Earn tokens by clearing rounds (Easy 1, Medium 1.5, Hard 2, Freeplay 1.5 per round) and beating challenges. 🐸 Frog Collection Unlock new frogs to use in your games:
🏷️ Class Buffs Every frog has a class. Level up a class to buff ALL the frogs in it (3 levels each).
🌳 Perk Tree Starts from 3 roots and splits up. Dashed perks join two branches: you need BOTH perks above them. Hover a perk for details.
